# Task 4.2 Review-Correction Design

**Date:** 2026-08-04

**Status:** User-approved approach; written specification pending final user review

## 1. Context

Task 4.2 produced the initial passive driver in commit
`622abf4d22232ecdf3dfa831d02a985578a145f3`. Its exact five-file scope builds
cleanly under net10 and the CLR-v2/net35 compatibility project, the focused and
complete C# harnesses pass, and the complete offline Python suite passes.
Independent specification and platform reviews approved the implementation.

The independent test/mutation and shell/gate reviews rejected completion for
evidence-quality reasons:

1. M03 manufactured an unreachable private state by retaining a candidate while
   reflectively clearing only the neutral flag.
2. M07 removed a replacement-time candidate clear that was behaviorally
   redundant with the same callback's `NotInspected` clear, so only a private
   field assertion killed it.
3. M10 corrupted both replacement epoch and frame metadata, while its evidence
   stopped at the first epoch assertion.
4. The ignored forced-suite helper was executed without authenticating its
   recorded bytes immediately before every use.
5. Mutation patch bytes were not authenticated before application.
6. Final acceptance checked mutation evidence existence and first status line,
   but did not revalidate its hashes, build markers, named behavioral oracle,
   diagnostic exclusions, or unique ledger binding.

These findings do not demonstrate a defect in the committed driver behavior.
They demonstrate that part of the proof could accept structural false positives
or substituted ignored evidence.

## 2. Decision

Task 4.2 will receive a history-preserving descendant correction. Commit
`622abf4d22232ecdf3dfa831d02a985578a145f3` remains unchanged. Pre-existing
historical artifact files and ledger rows remain unchanged; the two ledgers are
append-only and receive only uniquely prefixed correction rows. The correction
will add:

1. this design commit;
2. a descendant executable-plan hardening commit, including an amended current
   Task 4.2 plan whose literal inputs bind a newly generated ignored correction
   brief;
3. a descendant test-only correction commit produced under a new behavioral
   RED/GREEN and mutation cycle; and
4. after review and controller replay, a tracked evidence-seal commit that binds
   the complete dynamic evidence set to immutable Git history.

No rebase, amend, squash, or deletion of historical evidence is permitted. Old
Task 4.2 mutation artifacts remain historical and are explicitly superseded for
completion. New artifacts use a correction-specific namespace and never
overwrite the originals.

If a later review rejects a correction round, history remains append-only. Every
round begins with a tracked plan-anchor commit. A gate or plan finding is handled
by that docs-only commit; a test finding is handled by the plan-anchor commit
followed by one optional commit limited to the two test paths. The exact round
tip is the optional test commit when present and otherwise the plan-anchor
commit. Reviews and replay are dispatched only from that clean round tip. Each
round uses the next monotonically increasing identifier (`r01`, `r02`, and so
on), a new brief, and new evidence paths. The final gate binds the complete
descendant chain and the last accepted round rather than assuming exactly one
plan or test commit.

The correction does not change the passive-driver architecture, production
behavior, `Program.cs` registrations, or the Task 5.1--5.3 ownership transfer.

## 4. Behavioral mutation correction

### 4.1 M03: complete replacement continuity

The reflected `candidate != null && neutralSeen == false` setup is removed.
The corrected oracle uses only the supported boundary flow:

1. start an initial epoch;
2. observe a neutral input;
3. record one candidate capture with signature bytes `S`;
4. replace the verified state during a later update;
5. allow that same replacement callback to complete as `NotInspected`;
6. observe a new neutral input in the replacement epoch; and
7. require two fresh captures, each also with signature bytes `S`, before
   Initial can settle.

M03 becomes one explicitly documented, single-semantic coupled mutant. It
removes both redundant candidate-continuity breakers:

- the candidate clear in `StartInitialEpoch`; and
- the candidate clear in the `NotInspected` branch.

Each deletion alone is behaviorally equivalent for the supported replacement
callback. Together they express one fault: an old candidate survives the
complete replacement callback. The corrected supported-flow oracle must fail on
the first fresh-epoch capture with the unique fragment
`replacement requires two fresh candidate captures`.

This higher-order mutant is permitted only because its two hunks jointly model
one externally observable fault across redundant defenses. It must be applied
and reversed as one authenticated patch.

### 4.2 M07 and M10: independent rebase metadata

M07 becomes frame-only. It rebases the replacement directive to the correct new
epoch and frame zero:

```csharp
directive.RebaseInitialEpoch(epoch, 0);
```

It must fail at `replacement callback is frame one`.

M10 becomes epoch-only. It rebases to the old directive epoch and the correct
current frame:

```csharp
directive.RebaseInitialEpoch(directive.Epoch, currentFrames);
```

It must fail at `replacement directive uses new epoch`.

No mutation may combine these independent faults.

### 4.3 Removed private-state assertions

The correction removes:

- `SetNeutralSeenForDefensiveTest`;
- `AssertCandidateClearedForDefensiveTest`; and
- every call to those helpers.

The corrected M03, M07, and M10 evidence must fail through supported callback
behavior and public/internal record or directive outcomes, never through a
private candidate or neutral-field assertion.

## 5. Explicitly retained white-box coverage

Not all reflection in the test support has the defect above.

### 5.1 Frame pre-overrun defense

The state `AwaitInitialNeutral`, `currentFrames == max`, and no outstanding
directive cannot arise after a correctly completed exact-limit callback under
the supported serialized integration contract. Nevertheless, the approved
Task 4.2 design explicitly requires the defensive `nextFrames > max` branch,
path/gate/capture suppression, and capped error fields.

The frame injection used by M14 and M15 is therefore retained and explicitly
classified as **defensive invariant-violation coverage**, not as a claim that
the injected state is naturally reachable. Those mutants are acceptable only
because their assertions observe driver behavior after injection—call
suppression, terminal code, and capped record fields—and do not pass merely by
finding or inspecting a private field.

Removing that defensive branch would also require a coordinated amendment to
Task 5.1 and is outside this correction.

### 5.2 Authorization-before-gate observation

M24 is retained. Its probe passively reads the real directive stage during the
real `IsQuiescent` callback. It does not manufacture driver state. The observed
stage distinguishes the approved runtime order:

```text
AuthorizeUpdate -> IsQuiescent
```

from M24's incorrect order:

```text
IsQuiescent -> AuthorizeUpdate
```

The Task 4.1 directive-stage semantics are already part of the approved
contract. Adding a new production-only-for-tests authorization seam would
increase runtime surface without improving the behavioral distinction.

## 6. Strict correction RED/GREEN

The first correction round is `r01` and uses this exact new evidence namespace:

```text
task-4.2-correction-r01-red-M03-output.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-red-M03-status.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-red-M07-output.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-red-M07-status.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-red-M10-output.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-red-M10-status.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-implementer-MNN-output.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-implementer-MNN-status.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-controller-MNN-output.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-controller-MNN-status.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-preseal-output.txt
task-4.2-correction-r01-preseal-status.txt
```

Historical `task-4.2-MNN-*` files remain untouched and are not accepted as
correction evidence.

Each round's tracked plan contains an exact, phase-aware accepted-round artifact
manifest. It lists that round's brief, helper when round-specific, patches,
three RED pairs, 25 implementer pairs, 25 controller pairs, and pre-seal pair;
the shared helper and two ledgers are authenticated separately. Before an
output/status capture, its manifest entries are reserved outputs and must be
absent and non-symlinks. After a successful capture, they become materialized
inputs for every later gate. In particular, immediately before the pre-seal
capture, every earlier entry is materialized but the pre-seal pair remains
reserved. Any requirement to inspect "every" ignored artifact means every
materialized path in this manifest plus the named shared artifacts—not every
file under the ignored task directory. Prior-round files, including partial
pairs from a rejected round, remain present as preserved historical artifacts
but are unreferenced by the accepted manifest and cannot satisfy, invalidate, or
substitute for any accepted-round path or ledger key.

The controller generates the correction brief at
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-07-31-oracle-passive-plugin/task-4.2-review-correction-r01-brief.md`.
Corrected mutation patches use
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-07-31-oracle-passive-plugin/task-4.2-correction-r01-MNN.patch`.
The authoritative ledgers remain
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-07-31-oracle-passive-plugin/task-4.2-report.md` and
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-07-31-oracle-passive-plugin/progress.md`. Every new
correction row uses a unique `Task 4.2 correction r01` prefix.

Before each round's plan-anchor commit, the controller records each ledger's
then-current complete byte length and SHA-256 in that tracked plan. The recorded
bytes include every prior round row, even rows from a rejected round that never
received a seal. After committing the anchor and before appending any row for
the new round, the controller requires each complete ledger to equal its
recorded length/hash pair. That complete file is the immutable prefix for the
round; every later gate hashes its first recorded number of bytes and requires
all new bytes to follow it. Thus no prior unsealed row falls between protected
prefixes. The seal records the accepted round's anchor-prefix length/hash pairs,
the original historical-prefix pairs, and both final full-file hashes.

The `r01` historical boundaries are literal:

- `task-4.2-report.md`: 27,729 bytes, SHA-256
  `ee2a9115413771b9dd98f882583fbddabca6c84cc722369eee640c7d9dd86e00`;
- `progress.md`: 66,211 bytes, SHA-256
  `7e9c3a2c7a6f732ceb70f5cd0783bb4561c40e24ee84603e4703c363fcd6e54c`.

A later round substitutes its exact round identifier in every path and ledger
key. Its tracked plan anchor contains the literal current full-ledger prefix
pairs, expected brief/helper/patch bytes, and, for a test-changing round, the
expected post-correction hashes of both test files before the optional test
commit is created. Evidence from different rounds is never combined to satisfy
one gate.

The round sequence is:

1. pin the immutable implementation commit, correction design, executable plan,
   correction brief, current production hashes, and pre-correction test hashes;
2. in a test-changing round only, install exactly the two plan-pinned corrected
   test files; a gate-only round keeps its clean plan anchor unchanged;
3. demonstrate behavioral RED by applying the authenticated corrected M03,
   M07, and M10 patches independently, capturing one distinct RED output/status
   pair per mutation, and requiring each exact new behavioral failure;
4. reverse each patch before inspecting or accepting its evidence;
5. run fresh focused, complete, net35, and offline Python GREEN suites against
   the unmutated implementation;
6. execute all 25 corrected implementer mutations independently; and
7. after every precommit gate passes, create the optional test-only correction
   commit for a test-changing round; for a gate-only round, retain the clean plan
   anchor as the round tip without creating a test commit.

A gate-only round still reruns all three RED captures, every GREEN suite, and all
25 implementer mutations from its clean plan-anchor baseline. It does not reuse
prior-round dynamic evidence merely because the tracked test bytes are already
correct.

Because production behavior is unchanged, the RED is mutation-based: the new
tests must reject each corrected semantic fault while the exact unmutated
implementation stays GREEN.

Every round declares its exact Git plan anchor, optional test commit topology,
resulting round tip, and implementer restoration baseline before its first
mutation. For `r01`, that baseline is the plan anchor plus exactly the two
uncommitted corrected-test paths. A later test-changing round uses its newly
declared test diffs and ends at their optional test commit; a gate-only round
uses a clean committed baseline and ends at its plan anchor. Controller replay
always begins from the clean accepted round tip, which is the final pre-seal
correction commit. No gate infers a baseline merely from the word `implementer`
or assumes that every later round changes tests.

## 7. Evidence authentication

### 7.1 Forced-suite helper

The helper remains ignored, but every invocation must first require:

- a regular file;
- no symbolic link;
- the literal expected SHA-256 recorded in the commit-pinned correction plan;
  and
- the same helper hash in both evidence ledgers.

The helper path remains
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-07-31-oracle-passive-plugin/task-4.2-forced-suite.sh`.
Its approved unchanged body has SHA-256
`627b108d9c95600f966b00a2517788774cda1eb8bf54b2f4bf4ef56cc9572224`.
That historical path and body are immutable. If a later correction round needs
a different helper body, it creates
`task-4.2-correction-rNN-forced-suite.sh` under the same ignored task directory
and binds its new literal hash in the plan, round brief, both ledgers, and seal.

The type and hash checks occur immediately before every execution in pre-stage,
pre-commit, postcommit, and final acceptance gates. Recording the hash without
checking it is insufficient.

### 7.2 Mutation patches

The corrected plan contains the canonical body and literal SHA-256 for every
M01--M25 patch. Every implementer and controller transaction requires:

- a regular non-symlink file;
- the exact literal SHA-256;
- a successful forward `git apply --check` immediately before forward
  application;
- the exact phase-specific mutated tracked path set after forward application;
- a repeated regular-file, non-symlink, and literal SHA-256 authentication
  immediately after evidence capture and before any reverse operation;
- a successful reverse `git apply -R --check` on the mutated tree immediately
  before the inverse; and
- the exact phase-specific restored state after the inverse.

The inverse always occurs before the controller inspects or accepts captured
evidence. A reverse check on the pre-mutation tree is neither required nor
accepted as proof of reversibility.

Under each correction-round prefix, both ledgers contain exactly one patch-hash
row per mutation. Final acceptance recomputes each accepted-round patch hash and
matches it to the commit-pinned plan and the unique matching row under that
round's prefix in both ledgers.

### 7.3 Mutation outputs and statuses

Each implementer and controller evidence pair is separately bound in both
ledgers. Before capture, both paths must be absent and non-symlinks. Bash 3.2
`set -o noclobber` remains active for both writes, and `set +e` remains active
until both the output and one-line status are written. A partial pair is invalid
and is never overwritten or reused; a new correction round receives new paths.
Evidence hashes and ledger rows are created only after the authenticated inverse,
exact restoration, and fresh focused GREEN.

Throughout this correction, **Git-clean** means an empty index, no tracked
worktree diff, and no non-ignored untracked paths. It does not require deleting
ignored build products or preserved historical evidence. Ignored evidence paths
are outside the Git-clean predicate and are instead governed by the
phase-aware accepted-round manifest and named shared-artifact checks.

Every literal executable helper, gate, ledger-prefix check, evidence-seal
writer, closure check, and terminal-verifier block in the correction plan must
parse under Apple's `/bin/bash` 3.2.57 and execute using only constructs and
utilities available on macOS Tahoe 26.6. Each block is checked with
`/bin/bash -n` before use. Bash-4-only syntax and GNU-only flags or utilities are
forbidden; this requirement applies to the whole hardened workflow, not only to
output capture.

Final acceptance requires, for both accepted-round roles (`implementer` and
`controller`) and every M01--M25:

- regular non-symlink output and status files;
- a nonempty output file;
- exactly one status line equal to `1`;
- actual output and status SHA-256 values equal to unique matching rows in both
  ledgers;
- exactly one successful forced build with zero warnings and zero errors;
- an exact first-line role marker containing the accepted correction round,
  `implementer` or `controller`, and the mutation ID;
- the exact registered test and first-failure fragment for that mutation;
- absence of compiler, warning, SDK, assets, restore, timeout, permission,
  package, and infrastructure failure markers; and
- an authenticated inverse followed by exact production/test hashes and a fresh
  focused GREEN.

For an implementer pass, inverse restoration means an empty index and the exact
round-specific tracked and non-ignored-untracked baseline recorded before its
first mutation, with no additional tracked change or non-ignored untracked
path. For controller replay from the final pre-seal correction commit, inverse
restoration means a Git-clean repository. Required ignored evidence and
irrelevant ignored build products do not make either state dirty; the evidence
manifest authenticates the former separately. In particular, `r01` must not
require a Git-clean implementer worktree while its intended test-first
transaction remains uncommitted, while a later gate-only round must not invent
test diffs.

An output/status pair is never accepted merely because it exists or starts with
`1`.

The three correction RED pairs for M03, M07, and M10 receive the same
regular-file, non-symlink, nonempty-output, one-line-status, actual-hash,
role-marker, clean-build, named-oracle, diagnostic-rejection, inverse, and
phase-specific restoration checks. Each RED pair has a unique ledger row and is
revalidated before sealing and during final acceptance; no aggregate or shared
RED transcript is permitted.

Each RED output begins with the exact marker
`Task 4.2 correction rNN red MNN`; implementer and controller outputs use
`Task 4.2 correction rNN implementer MNN` and
`Task 4.2 correction rNN controller MNN`, respectively.

The two authoritative ledger paths must be distinct regular, non-symlink files.
Before the evidence seal, the controller requires their correction keys to be
unique and their values to agree, then records each complete ledger's SHA-256 in
the seal. Each round's plan declares each ledger's exact current-round suffix
schema, complete key set, and canonical order. Before freezing, the controller
requires every byte after that round's anchored prefix to conform to its declared
schema and rejects missing, duplicate, unexpected, out-of-order, or unparsed
current-round rows or bytes. The sealed ledger byte lengths and hashes are frozen
for the accepted round. A later round may append only after proving that each
entire prior sealed file is an unchanged prefix; the later seal then records new
full-file lengths and hashes.

## 8. Commit and evidence binding

Before sealing, both ledgers uniquely bind:

- original execution base and plan commit;
- immutable implementation commit `622abf4d22232ecdf3dfa831d02a985578a145f3`;
- correction design commit;
- every append-only correction-round plan and test commit;
- the exact final pre-seal correction commit;
- exact parent, subject, and per-commit tracked scope throughout the descendant
  chain;
- correction design, plan, brief, helper, patch, production, and test hashes;
- all three correction RED results;
- every corrected implementer mutation and GREEN result;
- all four reviewer identities and verdicts;
- every controller replay and restoration result; and
- one pre-seal candidate-acceptance result.

All key prefixes are unique, lowercase hashes have exact lengths, and values in
the two ledgers must match repository state. Neither ledger contains the later
seal commit or post-seal terminal-verifier result; attempting to append those
results would invalidate the sealed ledger hashes.

## 9. Independent review and controller replay

After the current round reaches its exact clean round tip—the optional test
commit for a test-changing round or the plan anchor for a gate-only round—four
fresh read-only reviewers examine the complete correction chain:

1. specification and Task 5.1--5.3 scope transfer;
2. behavioral tests, all mutation patches, and evidence freshness;
3. shell portability, authentication, and fail-closed final gates; and
4. C# 7.3, CLR-v2/net35, and macOS Tahoe 26.6 compatibility.

Every review must contain no Critical or Important finding. A rejected review
starts a new append-only correction round; approval never substitutes for a
failing gate.

Only after all four approvals may the controller independently replay all 25
corrected patches from the exact final pre-seal correction commit. Reviewers do
not create controller evidence. Each replay uses new correction-specific paths
and the same authenticated patch, role-marker, named-oracle, inverse,
restoration, and focused-GREEN requirements as the implementer pass.

After replay, a pre-seal candidate-acceptance gate revalidates the complete
commit chain, every materialized input in the accepted-round artifact manifest,
the shared helper and both ledgers, all reviews, and a fresh forced suite. It
simultaneously requires the manifest's reserved pre-seal output/status paths to
be absent and non-symlinks. Retained prior-round artifacts outside that manifest
are neither accepted nor rejected merely for existing. The gate keeps Bash 3.2
`set -o noclobber` active while capturing both reserved paths. The output must be
nonempty and have the exact first line `Task 4.2 correction rNN preseal`; the
status must contain exactly one line equal to `0`. After that gate returns, the
controller authenticates those properties, hashes the pair, marks it
materialized, appends its unique rows to both ledgers, and runs a separate
uncaptured closure check. The closure check revalidates the now-complete
manifest, the shared helper, both final ledgers, the exact commit chain and
accepted round tip, and Git-clean state, including every regular non-symlink
type, actual hash, unique matching row, exact marker, exact one-line zero status,
and pre-seal gate semantics. Only then are the ledgers frozen.

## 10. Immutable evidence seal

Ignored artifacts and two ignored ledgers are not an immutable provenance
anchor. After the pre-seal candidate gate passes, the controller writes
`docs/superpowers/evidence/2026-08-04-task-4.2-review-correction-evidence.md`.
The tracked seal contains these literal rows:

- accepted correction round;
- exact full descendant commit chain through the final pre-seal correction
  commit;
- exact parent, subject, and path scope for every correction commit;
- design, plan, brief, helper, production, and corrected-test hashes;
- every M01--M25 patch path and hash;
- all three RED output/status paths, hashes, role markers, registrations, and
  first-failure fragments;
- all 50 implementer/controller output/status paths, hashes, role markers,
  registrations, and first-failure fragments;
- both original historical ledger-prefix length/hash pairs, both accepted-round
  plan-anchor full-prefix length/hash pairs, and both frozen full-ledger paths,
  byte lengths, and hashes;
- all four review identities and verdicts;
- the pre-seal candidate-gate output/status paths, hashes, exact marker, and
  one-line zero status; and
- the controller replay and pre-seal full-suite summaries.

Before writing the seal, the controller recomputes every file-derived value from
its declared regular, non-symlink path and every Git-derived value from the
pinned Git object. Review-derived values come from four fresh read-only reviewer
returns that name the exact accepted round tip, and are cross-checked against
their unique matching rows in both ledgers. The controller rejects a missing or
extra entry within the exact accepted-round manifest and rejects duplicate-key,
cross-role, or cross-round evidence. Files retained from prior rounds are outside
that manifest and may remain on disk, but no value from them may enter the
accepted seal. Implementer and controller outputs carry different exact
first-line role markers, which prevents direct unedited cross-role copying and
accidental path reuse.

Controller honesty is the provenance trust boundary. A controller able to edit
all ignored artifacts, both ledgers, and the tracked seal could fabricate a
self-consistent local history; local self-hashes cannot prove execution against
that actor. The seal is designed to prevent accidental, stale, cross-round, and
post-seal substitution and to make review reproducible. Stronger adversarial
provenance would require an external signed attestation or independently
observed live replay and is outside the approved practical threat model.

The seal commit has subject `docs: seal Task 4.2 correction evidence`, has the
final pre-seal correction commit as its parent, and changes exactly the one seal
path. The seal does not contain its own blob hash or commit hash and therefore
has no self-reference. A later correction round replaces the tracked seal in a
new append-only seal commit while preserving the earlier seal in Git history.

## 11. Post-seal terminal verifier

The literal terminal verifier runs from the seal commit and does not write to
the frozen ledgers. It requires:

1. exact seal commit subject, parent, and one-file scope;
2. a regular non-symlink tracked seal;
3. exact recomputation of every accepted-round and shared ignored artifact and
   frozen-ledger hash named in the seal, without treating unreferenced
   prior-round files as accepted evidence;
4. exact role, round, registration, first-failure, build-marker, status, and
   diagnostic checks for all three RED pairs and all 50 mutation pairs;
5. a regular non-symlink pre-seal output/status pair with nonempty output, the
   exact first-line round marker, exactly one status line equal to `0`, actual
   hashes equal to the seal and unique matching frozen-ledger rows, and every
   semantic property required by the closure check;
6. exact production/test/plan/design/brief/helper/patch hashes;
7. a Git-clean repository;
8. a fresh authenticated forced net10/net35/focused/full/Python suite; and
9. after that suite, a second complete execution of items 1--6, including exact
   equality of `HEAD` to the initially recorded seal commit, its subject, parent,
   and one-file scope, plus every regular/non-symlink type, exact
   accepted/shared ignored-artifact hash, frozen-ledger hash, and semantic
   check; and
10. a final Git-clean repository.

The terminal verifier's exit status and live command output are intentionally
external terminal evidence. They are not appended to the sealed ledgers and do
not create a recursive requirement for a second seal. Completion is claimed
only from a fresh verifier exit of zero observed after the seal commit.

## 12. Completion criteria

Task 4.2 correction is complete only when:

1. original implementation history remains intact;
2. tracked correction scope is exact;
3. M03, M07, and M10 fail through the corrected behavioral oracles;
4. all 25 implementer mutations and all 25 controller replays are authenticated
   and killed at their named oracle;
5. every inverse restores exact final hashes and its exact phase-specific
   repository state—the declared round baseline during each implementer pass and
   a Git-clean repository at the exact accepted round tip during controller
   replay;
6. all four independent reviews approve without Critical or Important findings;
7. the tracked evidence seal binds every accepted ignored artifact, both frozen
   ledgers, every review, and the exact final pre-seal correction commit;
8. fresh forced net10/net35 builds have zero warnings and errors;
9. focused and complete C# harness output is exact;
10. the complete offline Python suite passes with exactly 1822 passed, 120
    xfailed, and 6 xpassed; and
11. the hardened post-seal terminal verifier exits zero from the exact evidence
    seal commit.

No real-game launch or manual level verification is required for this
evidence-only correction.
